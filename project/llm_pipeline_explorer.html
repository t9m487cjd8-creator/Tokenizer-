What you learn: computers read numbers, not words. A vocabulary is a dictionary that gives every token an ID. ID 0 is [UNK] (unknown). A new word is broken into smaller pieces (z ##e ##b ##r ##a), so the model never gets stuck. A smaller vocabulary means more pieces, so more tokens.

What you learn: embeddings turn sentences into points in 3D space. Spelling-based embeddings group words that look alike ('cat' and 'bat'), while meaning-based embeddings group sentences with similar ideas ('cat' and 'kitten', or 'stock market' and 'share prices') even when they share no words.
